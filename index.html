01Home

This is Priyanka. She lives in Gurugram and designs the screens people tap every day.

Three years in, she runs UI/UX projects end to end, across B2B and B2C.

02Study

It started at NIFT, with a Bachelor of Design, 2021 to 2024.

Wireframes, user flows and journey maps. Then the Google UX Design Professional Certificate on top.

03First briefs

Her first briefs came from Times Internet and Myntra.

Emailers, marketing campaigns and Lottie motion. Then campaign microsites and seasonal launches, laid out from the analytics.

04Mumbai

At Nykaa Fashion, she became a full-time UI designer.

Landing pages, A/B tests, prototypes, and accessibility checked against WCAG.

05Noise

Today she’s Assistant Manager, Product Designer at Noise.

The whole design lifecycle, a design system, and apps for iOS, Android and the web. Mentoring interns, scoping with product, building next to engineering.

06Now

And she builds with AI.

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, UX Pilot and Midjourney sit right next to Figma.

“I design simple things, and use AI to get there faster.”
